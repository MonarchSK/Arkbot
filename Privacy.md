Privacy Policy for Arkbot (Chill-Verse)
1. Information We Collect (And What We Don't)
Arkbot is built to respect your privacy and collects only the absolute minimum data required to run our community features. We collect:

Discord User IDs & Usernames: Stored securely to track your XP, rank tiers, and active cooldowns.

Voluntary Inputs: Your Date of Birth (only if you submit it via the verification or birthday modals) and the text of your Anonymous Confessions.

Server Activity: Message timestamps (to calculate XP cooldowns and trigger random Super Drops) and logs of deleted/edited messages for moderation purposes.

What We DO NOT Collect: Arkbot does not collect, process, or store your IP address, passwords, direct messages (outside of bot commands), or activity in other Discord servers.

2. How We Use Your Data
The data we collect is isolated to this server and used strictly for community features, including:

Operating the leveling ecosystem and automatically assigning rank roles.

Managing the community birthday calendar and automated announcements.

Processing and posting anonymous confessions.

Generating internal moderation audit logs for the High Command team.

3. Data Storage and Sharing
Your privacy is our priority. We do not sell, monetize, or share your data with any third-party services, advertisers, or data brokers. All data (XP, birthdays, confessions) is stored locally on the bot's secure host machine in isolated database files.

4. User Rights & Data Deletion
You have complete control over your participation. If you wish to have your data wiped—including your birthday, XP profile, or specific confessions—you can request a data deletion at any time. Simply open a ticket in the 🎫・tickets channel or ping a Server Administrator, and your data will be permanently purged from the bot's memory
